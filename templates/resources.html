General tips

  • Start with basics: cleanser, moisturizer, and sunscreen.
  • Patch-test new products on a small area first.
  • Give products time (often 4–6 weeks) before judging them.
  • Pay attention to ingredient lists, not just marketing.
  • Everyone’s skin is different, so adjust as needed.
